Our Story

Two College Students, One Simple Mission

We're San Diego locals who started SD Screen Pros because we noticed a gap — window rescreening shouldn't cost a fortune, and it shouldn't require waiting weeks for a big company to show up.

As college students here in San Diego, we know what it means to be on a budget. We built this business to offer quality craftsmanship at honest prices — no corporate markup, no upsells, just clean screens done right.

We serve all of San Diego County and take real pride in every job. When we show up at your home, you're not getting a random technician from a call center — you're getting us, and we care about the work we do.
